# I Love to Hang Out with My Friends

Report for production of an original Future House electronic song

Author: Bohan Jin

Date: September 22, 2025

## 1. Creative Background

### 1.1 Origin & Composition Progress

This composition originated from the inspiration of ROQUA’s Summer of the Beach. The foundational rhythm and groove were first built by the main leads of the song. With the rhythm in mind, other decorative tones for the lead and more synthesizers for chords were added and layered upon the lead. An elastic-tone bass and a sub-bass were added into the composition to make the music sound more energetic. Finally, drum and percussions samples were added to the composition to finalize the groove, and also allows chords to sidechain the kick to create the feeling of tides, which creates the fundamental rhythmic foundation of the music.

### 1.2 Experimental Elements

For this track, the incorporation of non-electronic sounds within parts that are heavily created with synthesized electronic sound is the experimental part. By using the non-electronic sounds, it allows us to add an extra layer to the music and also make the music sound more stereo and closer to nature.

### 1.3 Thematic Focus

The song tries to recreate the feeling of relax, happy, and energetic when I was having a good time with my friends. It presents a gradual emotional uplift to demonstrate the increasing excitement when being around with good friends, with the highest energy in the end to conclude this excitement and energy.

## 2. Composition

### 2.1 Production & Arrangement Refinement

Following the initial demo, the production process involved significant refinement of the sound selection and mixing. Initially, for the first drop, the tones for snares and kick do not nicely reflect the feeling of bouncy and energetic, which does not boost up the energy level in drop sufficiently and also makes the music sound static and soft. By switching the kick sample to Cymatics Ultimate Kick 2, which contains more low frequency and bouncier, it significantly boosts up the energy level of the song in the drop section and also balance the volume between higher frequencies and lower frequencies. Also, in order to add more layers of the sound, I also added more percussion to the drop section, but the mixing of the percussion also changed significantly along the composition process. RX 11 De-Reverb was applied to reduce the original reverb effects in the samples to make the percussion sounds more clear and also re-adjust the equalizer to make sure that the low frequencies sound from Conga inside the percussion does not overlap with other low frequencies from other instruments.

### 2.2 Melody Development

With the melody in the drop section, a temporary ending composed with grand piano was composed right after the first iteration of the drop section. It uses swing melody to make the ending happy and quick. With the ending in mind, the intro of the song was developed by rearranging the chord progressions and partial of the melodic line. This makes the ending call back from the intro and also allows easier development for the chorus section.

For the chorus section, simple, cute, and analog sounds were selected in priority in order to create a very relaxing and happy vibe and also allow me to use those sounds as the “singer” of the music by apply different modulations. Also, short notes with wide range of pitches is one of the principles while designing the melodic line for the chorus. This will make the music more energetic and mimicking human singing, which will fulfill the purpose of lead in this part.

### 2.3 Structure and Emotional Progression

The overall arrangement and emotional progression is aimed to gradually push up the energy level of this music. The main part of the song includes two chorus, two build ups, and two drops. In which the second build up and the second drop intentionally raise the energy level. For the second build up, instead of using the acoustic electric guitar as the lead, it directly uses synthesizer to boost the initial energy level. Also, during the build up process, the initial value of the low pass filter adjusted higher than the first build up to allow more energy entering this section. At the very beginning of the second drop, instead of directly entering, some simple analog arcade sound were used to work as a break section to increase audience’s expectation level, and in the second bar, after entering the real drop, this expectation got resolved, and allowed more energy to bump in to boost the whole song to the climax. Also, from the last few notes from the second build up, the scale of the song was also raised to correspond to the increment of energy level.

## 3. Mixing and Mastering Process

As an electronic music production, mixing and mastering played a key role in helping you to correctly express your opinion and emotions to the audience, and at the same time, to keep the music sounds quality. During the creation process, mixing occupies the majority of the time. For this music, the most fundamental and important mixing techniques used are auto filters and sidechaining. By automating the low-pass filter along with the sidechain applied to the instruments, the feeling of “tides” will be nicely constructed and provide extra energy and motivation to the music. Almost all instruments and synthesizers’ compressors were side-chained to the main kick to create a gain-reduction every time the kick was triggered. By doing this sidechaining, this also allows me to control the volume and clarity of each instrument, especially for parts like drops that the loudness is hard to control.

Also, when approaching the end of the production, I reviewed each of the instruments in the drop sections again to make sure that all of the sounds can be heard and remove unclear or not useful sounds. This process hugely improved the clarity of sounds in drops sections and also allowed the boost of energy became more obvious.

For the mastering section, the main goal is to control and normalize the loudness and also do some final EQ adjustments. Electronic music is a genre that has a higher loudness level than other genres in order to create great impact on the audience, hence the loudness of this music is controlled to about -10 LUFS instead of -14 LUFS. Plugins like Maximizer is also used to control the gain level and loudness. Also, some slightly EQ adjustment is also used in order to boost the high frequencies and low frequencies sounds to improve its impact.
